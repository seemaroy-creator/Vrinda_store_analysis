#  Vrinda_Store_Analysis
End-to-end Excel project focus on data cleaning, pivot table analysis, and data visualisation for business operations.

# Project objective
- Compare the sales and orders using chart.
- Which month got the highest sales and orders?
- Who Perchased more- men or women in 2022?
- What are the different order status in 2022?
- List top 10 states contributing to the sales?
- Relation between age and gender based on number of orders?
- Which channel is contributing to maximum sales?
- Highest selling category?

# Key Insights
- **Peak sales month:** March recorded the highest transactions.
- **Top buyers:** Women sales mojority of sales, contributing about **65%** of total purchases.
- **Top regions:** Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the three highest performing states
- **Top age group:** Young-Adults aged **30-49** years are the biggest buyers, bringing in nearly **50%** of sales.
- **Top channels:** Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra are the most successful sales platforms.

# Strategic Recommendations
To maximize growth, Vrinda Store should focus its marketing efforts on women aged 30- 49 living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.This can achieved by launching targeted ads, special offers, and shopping coupons directly on Amozon, Flipkart and Myntra.
